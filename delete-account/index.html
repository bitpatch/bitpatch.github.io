Delete your Conflict account and data

For the game Conflict by Bitpatch, on Android.

How to request deletion

  1. In Conflict, open Settings and find your player ID.
  2. Write an email to contact@bitpatch.dev with the subject Delete account.
  3. Put your player ID in the email. If you can't find it, write anyway and tell us roughly when you last played; we will help you find it.
  4. We reply to confirm the request, and we delete your data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • your account and player ID;
  • your gameplay statistics;
  • crash reports and diagnostics linked to your player ID or installation.

What may be kept

Aggregated statistics that are no longer linked to you, your player ID or your device, such as how many matches were played on a mission, may be kept indefinitely. They cannot be used to identify you.

If the law requires us to keep some data for longer, we keep only that data and only for as long as required.

Deleting only some data

If you want to keep your account but delete your gameplay statistics and crash reports, say so in the email, and we will delete only those.

Data on your device

Uninstalling the game removes everything it has stored on your device.
