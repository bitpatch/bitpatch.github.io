Privacy policy for Conflict

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Conflict, a mobile game by Bitpatch (“we”), collects and how it is used. It applies to the Android app com.bitpatch.conflict.

In short

  • The current version of Conflict does not collect, store or send any personal information.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.
  • Future versions may collect crash reports and anonymous gameplay statistics, only to fix problems and improve the game. We will never sell this data or use it for advertising.

What the game collects now

Nothing. Conflict does not ask you to sign in, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone, and does not contain advertising or analytics code. The game does not save personal information on your device.

The Unity engine

Conflict is built with the Unity engine. Unity may automatically send limited technical information to Unity Technologies, such as the device model, operating system version, and diagnostic data about engine crashes. This information is not used to identify you. Unity handles it under the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Conflict is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs, updates and app crashes under the Google Privacy Policy. We may see this information only in aggregated form in the Google Play Console.

Future versions: crash reports and analytics

Like most games, future versions of Conflict may collect:

  • crash and error reports: what went wrong, the app version, the device model and the operating system version;
  • gameplay statistics: for example, which missions are played, how long matches last and how they end;
  • a random identifier created for the installation of the game, so that statistics from one device can be counted together. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.

We would use this information only to find and fix problems and to improve the game and its balance. It would be collected by an analytics or crash reporting service acting on our behalf, and we will name that service in this policy. We do not sell this information, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone else, except when required by law.

Before a version of the game that collects this information is released, we will update this policy and the Data safety section of the game on Google Play.

Children

Conflict does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because the current version collects no personal information, there is nothing stored about you that we could show or delete. If future versions collect data, you will be able to ask us to delete the data linked to your installation by writing to the address below. Uninstalling the game removes everything it has stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@bitpatch.dev
