Privacy policy for Conflict

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Conflict, a mobile game by Bitpatch (“we”), collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Android app com.bitpatch.conflict.

In short

  • Conflict collects crash reports, gameplay statistics, an identifier of your installation and, once you have an account, your player ID.
  • We use this data to keep the game working, to fix problems and to improve the game, and to run your account and online play.
  • We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time: see Delete your account and data.

What we collect

  • Crash reports and diagnostics: what went wrong, the app version, the device model and the operating system version.
  • Gameplay statistics: what you do in the game, for example which missions you play, how long matches last and how they end.
  • Installation identifier: a random identifier created for your installation of the game, so that statistics and reports from one device can be counted together.
  • Player ID: the identifier of your account in the game. At first the game creates a guest account for you; later you will be able to sign in with Google Play Games, and the player ID will be linked to that sign-in.

Some of this is collected only by versions of the game that have the related features, such as accounts and online play.

Conflict does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or anything from your camera or microphone, and it contains no advertising.

Why we collect it

  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand how the game is played and to improve its missions and balance (analytics).
  • To run your account and online play (player ID).

This data is needed for the game to work as described, so its collection cannot be turned off in the game. If you do not want it collected, please do not use the game, and ask us to delete what has already been collected.

Who processes it

The data is collected by an analytics and crash reporting service that works on our behalf and may use the data only for us. We have not chosen the service yet; when we do, we will name it in this policy. Service providers like this are not third parties that we share your data with.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone else, except when required by law.

How we protect it

The data is encrypted in transit between the game and the service. Access to it is limited to the people who work on the game.

How long we keep it

We keep the data while it is needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it. Aggregated statistics that can no longer be linked to you or your device may be kept longer.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it by email. The steps are on the page Delete your account and data. Uninstalling the game removes everything it has stored on your device.

The Unity engine

Conflict is built with the Unity engine. Unity may automatically send limited technical information to Unity Technologies, such as the device model, the operating system version, and diagnostic data about engine crashes. Unity handles it under the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Conflict is distributed through Google Play, and you can sign in with Google Play Games. Google handles the information it collects under the Google Privacy Policy. We see Google Play's reports about installs and crashes only in aggregated form.

Children

Conflict does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information such as a name or an email address. If you believe we hold data about a child that should be deleted, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@bitpatch.dev
