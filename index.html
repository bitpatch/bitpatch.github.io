Bitpatch

A small independent game studio.

We make games that are simple to pick up and worth thinking about. Small team, few rules, careful details.

How we work

We work slowly and on purpose. An idea starts as a rough sketch, gets played, broken and rebuilt, and stays only if it still feels right a week later. Most ideas don't make it, and that is fine.

  • Simple rules, real decisions. A few clear rules can lead to choices that take a long time to master.
  • Quiet design. Nothing on the screen that doesn't need to be there.
  • Respect for your time. No dark patterns, no endless notifications, no tricks to keep you hooked.

Why “Bitpatch”

Because that is how things get made here: bit by bit, and better with every patch.
